01 — The Icon

More than football.

Cristiano Ronaldo's story is a combination of talent, discipline, sacrifice, ambition and an extraordinary ability to reinvent himself.

The mentality behind CR7

From Madeira to the biggest stages in world football, Ronaldo built a career around standards that rarely moved. Training, recovery, competition and an obsession with improvement became part of the identity.

The legacy is not only the goals or trophies. It is the example of what happens when ambition meets relentless work.

03 — Career Deep Dive

Six clubs. Six identities.

Ronaldo's career is best understood as a sequence of reinventions: every environment asked something different, and each chapter added a new layer to his game.

Early life & Sporting CP

Madeira was the starting point. Sporting CP became the bridge from youth football to elite competition, giving Ronaldo the platform from which Manchester United could recruit him as a teenager in 2003.

✦ Madeira → Lisbon was the first major move.
✦ Sporting's academy shaped his technical and athletic base.
✦ His performance against Manchester United became a decisive turning point.

Manchester United → global superstar

At Old Trafford he developed from a raw winger into a complete forward. The 2007/08 season became the breakthrough: 42 goals in all competitions, the Premier League title, the Champions League and the Ballon d'Or.

42 Goals · 2007/08
31 PL goals
1 UCL title
1 Ballon d'Or

Real Madrid — the scoring era

Real Madrid's official history records 451 goals in 438 official appearances. UEFA notes four Champions League wins with Madrid: 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018.

451 All-time Real Madrid goals.
438 Official appearances.
4 Champions League wins with Real Madrid.
105 Champions League goals during the Madrid period, per UEFA.

Juventus — adaptation in Italy

The Juventus chapter proved the scoring model could travel. Ronaldo won two Serie A titles, the Coppa Italia and finished as the league's top scorer in 2020/21.

2 Serie A titles.
1 Coppa Italia.
2020/21 Serie A top scorer.

Manchester United — the return

In 2021 Ronaldo returned to Old Trafford. His second debut against Newcastle produced two goals and immediately reconnected one of football's most recognisable players with his old club.

Al Nassr — a new football frontier

Ronaldo's Saudi chapter expanded the reach of his career beyond Europe. In 2025/26, Al Nassr won the Saudi Pro League for the first time since 2019, with Ronaldo scoring twice in the title-clinching final-day win over Damac.

11 — Portugal Legacy

The captain, the records.

Ronaldo's Portugal chapter includes EURO 2016, Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025, and global international scoring and appearance records.

Major tournament story

2004 EURO debut on home soil as a teenage international.
2016 Portugal become European champions.
2019 Portugal win the inaugural Nations League title.
2025 Portugal win the Nations League again, beating Spain on penalties.
2026 Scores at a sixth World Cup — a tournament record.

Latest international status

As of early October 2026, Ronaldo's future with Portugal is unresolved. Reuters reported that he left the national-team camp during the Nations League window after a dispute around his involvement; the federation and coach Jorge Jesus left the door open for a return.
